## r/hobbycnc (also r/CNC, r/PrintedCircuitBoard)

**Title:** I made a free app for milling PCBs on a hobby CNC: Gerber to G-code, 3D preview and auto-levelling

I mill my own circuit boards and got tired of juggling tools, so I built PCB TraceMill. It is free and open
source, and runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

- Import the Gerber and drill files from KiCad, EasyEDA, Altium or Eagle. The import shows how to export them
  from your program and which layers you really need.
- Press **Recipe** and you get isolation, drilling and cutout with tabs, with bits, depths and speeds chosen
  for you. The isolation depth comes from the narrowest copper gap on your board.
- Check everything in a 3D simulation before cutting.
- Auto-level with your probe: on Mach3 the heights stay in Mach3's own variables (no macros), GRBL and
  LinuxCNC probe logs load directly. Before each bit, Z0 is found on copper that is still connected to the
  probe clip.

Output for Mach3/Mach4, GRBL (Candle, UGS, bCNC, CNCjs) and LinuxCNC.

Website and download: https://aazriel.github.io/pcbtracemill/

I'd love feedback from people who mill boards on real machines: what works, what is missing?

## r/KiCad

**Title:** Free tool to mill your KiCad boards at home on a hobby CNC (Gerber → G-code, 3D preview, auto-levelling)

For those who prototype on a CNC instead of ordering boards: PCB TraceMill reads KiCad's fabrication outputs
(F.Cu, B.Cu, Edge.Cuts and the Excellon drill files) and writes G-code for Mach3, GRBL or LinuxCNC. The import
walks you through the KiCad export (File → Fabrication Outputs → Gerbers, then Generate Drill Files) and tells
you which layers are required, recommended or optional.

It handles isolation with depth from the measured copper gaps, drilling and milling big holes, cutout with
tabs and double-sided boards with alignment pins, with a 3D simulation of the milled board before you cut.

Free and open source (MIT), Windows / macOS / Linux.
Guide: https://aazriel.github.io/pcbtracemill/guide.html
Source: https://github.com/aazriel/pcbtracemill

## Hackaday tip line (hackaday.com/submit-a-tip)

**Subject:** Free, open-source PCB milling CAM with a 3D preview and macro-free Mach3 auto-levelling

PCB TraceMill is a free, open-source (MIT) desktop app that turns KiCad, EasyEDA or Altium Gerber and drill
files into G-code for hobby CNC routers running Mach3, GRBL or LinuxCNC. Isolation depth is derived from the
narrowest copper gap on the board, narrow gaps get a single groove down the middle, and a ray-marched 3D
simulation shows the milled board before cutting. On Mach3, probed heights stay in Mach3's own variables, so
auto-levelling needs no macros or files, and each bit finds Z0 on copper still connected to the probe clip.
The new import guide shows how to export the right layers from each design program. Windows, macOS, Linux.
https://aazriel.github.io/pcbtracemill/ · https://github.com/aazriel/pcbtracemill
