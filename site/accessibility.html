Accessibility statement

Last updated: 24 November 2026

We want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website. It is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 of the W3C at level AA, which also covers WCAG 2.0 AA and the national standards built on it.

What we did

  • Semantic page structure with ordered headings, marked navigation and content areas, and the page language set.
  • A “Skip to content” link at the top of every page, and full use with the keyboard alone (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter).
  • A clearly visible yellow outline around whatever has keyboard focus.
  • Colour contrast that meets level AA, and links inside text that are underlined, not shown by colour only.
  • Text alternatives for every meaningful image and screenshot.
  • A layout that adapts to any screen (computer, tablet, phone) and works at 200% zoom without losing content.
  • No animation or smooth scrolling when “reduce motion” is set in the operating system.
  • Questions and answers that open and close with standard elements that screen readers understand.
  • No accessibility overlay plugin: accessibility is built into the site itself.

How we checked

Automated testing with axe-core against WCAG 2.0 and 2.1 (levels A and AA), and manual keyboard testing, in current Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome. The site uses standard HTML and CSS that Firefox and Safari also support.

Known limits and third-party content

  • Screenshots of the app contain small text. Each has a text description, and the page text and the guide explain them.
  • Some parts are provided by other services and are not fully in our control: downloads and issue reports (GitHub), the reviews area (giscus and GitHub Discussions) and donations (Ko-fi). Their accessibility is the responsibility of those providers.
  • This statement covers the website. The PCB TraceMill desktop app is separate software.

We keep improving the site. If something is hard to use, please tell us so we can fix it.

Contact

Found an accessibility problem, or need information from the site in another form? Please tell us through the accessibility contact form with the page address, what went wrong, and the browser and assistive technology you use (if any). We aim to reply within 14 days.
