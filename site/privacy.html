Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: this website and the PCB TraceMill app do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and do not ask you for an account or any personal details. Your PCB files never leave your computer. A few things on the site are provided by other services, which are listed below.

This policy is written to meet common privacy laws, including the EU / UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

The website and the app are run by the PCB TraceMill project, maintained by an individual developer. You can reach us through the privacy contact form.

The website

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you open a page, and may keep them in its server logs for security. We do not have access to these logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Download counter. To show how many times the app was downloaded, your browser asks the public GitHub API for the release list. Only that number is stored, in your own browser (localStorage), for one hour. It is not a cookie and identifies nobody.
  • Reviews. The reviews area uses giscus, which shows comments from this project’s GitHub Discussions. It loads only after you press Show reviews; your browser then remembers that choice (localStorage). Reading is anonymous. To write a review you sign in to GitHub through giscus; your review, GitHub user name and picture are then public on GitHub. You can edit or delete your review on GitHub at any time. See the giscus privacy policy.
  • Bug reports and ideas are posted on GitHub Issues and are public. Please do not include private details.
  • Donations are made on Ko-fi, not on this site; this website never sees your payment details. Ko-fi and its payment providers (PayPal, Stripe) process the payment. We receive from Ko-fi the name, e-mail address and message you choose to give, and use them only to thank you and to keep the records the law requires (for example for tax). We never sell or share them. See the Ko-fi privacy policy.
  • Links to other websites (YouTube, forums, design programs) follow those websites’ own policies.

The PCB TraceMill app

  • The app works offline. Your design files, projects and G-code stay on your computer.
  • To tell you about new versions, the app asks the public GitHub API which version is the latest (Help → Check for Updates…). No personal data and no file content is sent; GitHub sees your IP address as with any web request.
  • The app has no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you (in practice: donor details from Ko-fi), as privacy laws such as the GDPR provide. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us through the privacy contact form (if your request includes private details, ask us there for a private way to reach us).

Children

The site is a technical resource for hobbyists and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
