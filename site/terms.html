CNC safety: you control the machine

PCB TraceMill writes G-code; your machine and you carry it out. Machining can damage the machine, the workpiece and the bits, and can injure people. Before you run a file:

  • Check it in the simulation, and if in doubt run it in the air (raised Z) first.
  • Make sure the machine, its limits, the units, the zero point and the bits match what the files expect.
  • Stay at the machine while it runs, keep an emergency stop within reach, and never put hands near a turning bit.
  • Wear eye protection. Milling FR4 makes fine glass-fibre and epoxy dust that is harmful to breathe: use dust extraction or a suitable mask, and clean up with a vacuum, not compressed air.
  • Follow your machine maker’s safety instructions and the rules of your country.
